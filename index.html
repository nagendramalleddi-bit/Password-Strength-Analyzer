What you learn with this tool

🔢

Entropy & Pool Size

Entropy (bits) = length × log₂(pool). Larger pools and longer passwords exponentially increase crack resistance.

🧂

Hashing & Salting

This tool stores a one-way hash of your password — never the raw text. Real systems use bcrypt/Argon2 with salts.

⚡

Brute-force Attacks

Modern GPUs can test 10B+ guesses/sec. Crack time estimates show why 8-char passwords aren't enough.

🔄

Password Reuse

Reusing passwords across sites is the #1 risk. Hash-based logging detects reuse without exposing your actual password.
